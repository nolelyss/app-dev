> "Little turtle, lost at sea..." — Jessica Santiago

## Movie Info
* **Director:** Jerry Lopez Sineneng
* **Genre:** Drama / Family
* **Main Theme:** Family, guilt, and finding where you belong.

## Why I Like This Movie
1. **Jessica's Story:** I really relate to Jessica Santiago. She wrote the poem *"Little Turtle Lost at Sea"* as a reflection of her own inner pain and feelings of being lost.
2. **The Quote:** The line "little turtle, lost at sea" captures the heavy feeling of floating in an ocean of grief while searching for home and comfort.
3. **The Relationship:** It is a touching story about sisters navigating trauma, forgiveness, and finding their way back to each other.
4. **The Ending Line:** *"Little turtle lost at sea / You will be found...maybe"*
   * **Meaning:** This line reveals her deepest internal struggle. The word *"maybe"* shows the quiet hope and desperation for someone to notice her pain and find her, even while doubting it will happen.
